# QML Basics

In this chapter you will create your first application with QML. You will explore the basic structure of a QML file and learn how to place your first elements in a window. You will also learn the difference between an item's implicit size and its actual size, and start using your first positioners.
And, because practice makes perfect (or as the French say, *c'est en forgeant qu'on devient forgeron*), you will be invited along the way to experiment with QML on your own.

```admonish warning "Your screen may look different"
From now on, this course includes screenshots of windows, buttons and other visual elements. What you see on your screen may look a bit different depending on your operating system (Windows, Linux, macOS…) and your settings (light or dark theme, etc.).
This is expected: by default, QML tries to match the look and feel of your system. The behavior is the same; only the appearance changes.
```
